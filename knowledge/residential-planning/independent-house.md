# Independent house program
A parking ground floor contains vehicle bays, pedestrian access and vertical circulation. Do not add bedrooms, kitchens or residential halls without an explicit request for mixed use.
Public living floors group living, kitchen and dining. Sleeping floors prioritize bedroom privacy and direct access from a shared landing. Do not use a bedroom as the route to another room.
G+3 is four occupied levels. An open terrace above G+3 is a fifth level. Preserve the third floor when adding a terrace.
Shared bathrooms and en-suite bathrooms are separate requirements. Never subtract an explicitly requested shared bathroom because a bedroom has an attached bathroom.
A floor not described by the customer is an unresolved requirement; ask for its purpose rather than filling it with arbitrary rooms.
