# Compact sites, parking, lifts and roof leisure
These are concept-planning heuristics, not local building code.
A lift, stair and their approach areas consume a substantial part of a 20 by 30 foot plot. Do not promise a large master bedroom and dedicated cinema until all room sizes pass the design rules.
Align staircase and elevator reservations on every floor. The elevator should stop only at the requested levels. Allow for supplier-specific pit, overhead and shaft construction later.
Keep a separate pedestrian route between the street, stairs and lift. A stationary car symbol is not proof that turning access works; road width and turning geometry need a further check.
A terrace jacuzzi needs structural load, waterproofing and drainage review. A covered shelter is a pergola/seating area, not an additional bedroom. Garden, jacuzzi and shelter are distinct requested uses and must all be represented.
